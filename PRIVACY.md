# Privacy Policy for FC-1000
**Effective Date:** August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **FC-1000** Discord bot ("the Bot") processes information. The Bot is a simple welcome tool designed solely to greet new members when they join a server.

### 1. Data We Process
The Bot does not look for, read, or collect any sensitive personal data. It only processes the following public Discord data in real-time:
* **Usernames and Mentions:** To say hello to the specific user who just joined.
* **Server/Channel IDs:** To know which welcome channel to send the greeting message into.

### 2. No Data Storage
We value your privacy. **FC-1000 does not own or use a database.** 
* No user data is ever written down, saved, or stored permanently.
* All data is processed instantly in-memory to send the welcome message and is immediately discarded.

### 3. Data Sharing
We do not store any data, so we have nothing to sell, trade, or share. No third-party services are used.

### 4. Contact and Support
If you have any questions about this Bot or its behavior, you can contact the developer by joining our official Discord support server:
* **Discord Support:** https://discord.gg/k6kj5kJjn
